Vitrina home screen — pick

Pick: v1 — "For Rent Sign".

The job: an agent or landlord, on a phone in front of a vacant space, sees in 5 seconds that it goes space in → ranked tenant types → a sheet to send, and can start right there.

  1. The value is clear in 5 seconds, using the agent's own object. Every agent knows the yellow FOR RENT sign. Here they fill in the sign itself, and its lines change to the tenant types that fit. The three-line claim ("Your space. Who'll rent it. On one page.") states the whole promise before anyone reads the body text. In English the sign works even better: FOR RENT in condensed capitals is the real sign, word for word.
  2. All three parts are above the fold at 390 px: the claim, the inputs (address, m², asking rent, plus a one-tap sample space), and a glimpse of the result. The sign's tear-off tabs show the sample ranking with real scores (Pharmacy 81 … Fast food 58), and tapping a tab jumps to the sheet.
  3. It passes the covered-logo test best of the five. Big Shoulders Display (condensed sign lettering), signal yellow on ink, and the sign as the signature look nothing like the other three sites. The sheet below ("And this is the sheet you send.") puts the deliverable first, with Send to owner / Download PDF next to it and a preview of the exact message.

Runner-up: v4 (message thread). It fits one-thumb phone use best, but the chat frame needs more reading before the value is clear, and its look is less ownable.

Borrow from v4: treat the sheet as something you forward. Put the agent's own name and phone on the sheet, and show the share confirmation as the attachment the owner will receive (preview card, delivery state), not just a text box.
